How it works

Every project moves through the same five stages, so you always know where the work stands.

Stage 1

Prepare

Upload a trial balance and general ledger, or follow the guided QuickBooks Online export. Cerdify checks the files and reconciles the trial balance to the ledger.

Stage 2

Diagnose

Accounting diagnostics flag data-quality and reconciliation issues, and explain why each one matters.

Stage 3

Support

Set a materiality threshold, get a versioned list of transactions that need evidence, and match documents to them.

Stage 4

Close

Period-close checks show what is supported, what is missing and what is unclear. Approved corrections post back to your books as journal entries.

Stage 5

Report

Export Excel and PDF reports that show the work performed, the open items and the limits of the review.

Built around evidence, not attachments.

Cerdify keeps the reasoning visible, so a reviewer can see why something passed, failed or is still open.

Evidence, piece by piece

For every transaction, Cerdify keeps these questions separate:

  • Is a document linked to it?
  • Is it the right kind of support?
  • How much of the amount does it cover?
  • What did the reviewer conclude?

Explainable results

See why an item was included, flagged or left undetermined. Missing evidence stays visible instead of disappearing into a single score.

A record of every decision

Review decisions keep who made them and when. Earlier versions are kept, so you can see how the close changed.

What Cerdify does

  • Checks trial balance and general ledger files before you start
  • Runs ledger and reconciliation diagnostics
  • Builds a versioned list of transactions that need support
  • Organizes supporting documents and reviewer conclusions
  • Flags period-close issues and proposes correcting journal entries
  • Posts approved entries to QuickBooks Online and confirms they match
  • Produces practical next steps and exports

What Cerdify does not do

  • Perform an audit, review, compilation or assurance engagement
  • Certify your books or guarantee an outcome
  • Replace your CPA, auditor, attorney, tax adviser or bookkeeper
  • Change your accounting records without a person's approval
  • Make a final judgment when evidence or review is incomplete

Questions

Is Cerdify an audit?

No. Cerdify is software that organizes a review of your books and the evidence behind them. It does not issue an audit opinion or provide assurance.

Do I need to connect QuickBooks Online?

No. You can upload a trial balance and general ledger, or follow the guided QuickBooks Online export. You stay in control of the files you provide.

What files do I start with?

A trial balance and general ledger for the same company, period and accounting basis. Cerdify checks them before the project is created.

Does a high score mean I'll pass an audit?

No. A score reflects the data, evidence and reviews available at the time. It is not a guarantee, a certification or a substitute for an auditor's work.

Can Cerdify post corrections to my books?

Yes, with your approval. Cerdify proposes a correcting journal entry, a reviewer approves it, and Cerdify posts it to QuickBooks Online, then reads it back to confirm it matches. Nothing is posted without a person's approval. Direct posting is being enabled for beta customers, and more accounting systems will follow.

How do I join the beta?

The beta is small and by invitation, for U.S. companies and the people who keep their books.
