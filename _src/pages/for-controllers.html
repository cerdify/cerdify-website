A balanced trial balance isn't an answer.

Close-management tools track whether tasks are done. The harder question is whether the numbers are right and supported. That is what Cerdify checks.

  • What supports the material balances this month?
  • Which exceptions are open, and who owns them?
  • Who approved this correcting entry, and when?
  • What changed since the version we reviewed last week?
  • What did the review not cover?

What you get each close

A consistent evidence process, run the same way every month.

Material gaps first

Set a materiality threshold. Cerdify builds a versioned list of transactions that need evidence and shows which are supported, partly supported or missing support.

Period-close checks

Depreciation, accruals, unrecorded bills, prepaid expenses, loan interest, payroll tax liabilities, bank and card balances, and entries that look duplicated, each explained in plain language.

A record of every decision

Review decisions and approvals keep who made them and when. Earlier versions are kept, so you can show how the close changed.

A report you can hand over

Export Excel and PDF reports that show the work performed, the open items and the limits of the review. The limits are stated as plainly as the results, so nobody reads more into the report than it says.

Approved corrections post to QuickBooks Online as journal entries, and Cerdify reads each one back to confirm it matches the approval. How posting works.

Clear about what it is

Cerdify is review software. It does not perform an audit, review or compilation engagement, and it does not certify the books. It helps you run your own review and keep the record of it.
