The work you redo every month

Most of a cleanup isn't the entries. It's finding out which transactions need support, chasing the client for it, and then checking that what they sent actually covers the amount.

  • Which transactions are big enough to need a document?
  • The client sent a receipt. Does it cover the whole bill?
  • Is this a statement, an invoice, or just an email?
  • Did depreciation, accruals and prepaid amortization post this month?
  • Is the bank balance in the books the one on the statement?

How Cerdify helps

The same five stages for every client, so the work looks the same whichever company you open.

A support list, not a pile of attachments

Set a materiality threshold and get the list of transactions that need evidence. Each one shows whether a document is linked, whether it's the right kind and how much of the amount it covers.

Know what to ask the client for

Missing and unclear evidence is named transaction by transaction, so the request to the client is specific: this vendor, this amount, this kind of document.

Fixes that post themselves

Period-close checks propose correcting journal entries. Once a reviewer approves one, Cerdify posts it to QuickBooks Online and confirms it matches.
