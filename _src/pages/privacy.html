Privacy Notice

Effective 23 November 2026 · Beta version

Cerdify Software, LLC provides software that helps businesses review their accounting records, organize supporting evidence and find gaps before the books are closed. This notice explains what personal information we handle and how. When we process a customer's accounting records on that customer's behalf, the customer decides why the records are processed, and our agreement with the customer also applies.

Questions or requests: support@cerdify.com.

1. Information we receive

  • Account and contact information: name, work email, organization, role, sign-in identifiers and support messages.
  • Accounting records you upload or connect: trial balances, general ledgers, bank statements, invoices and other supporting documents, along with the findings, reviews and exports Cerdify produces from them. These business records can identify customers, vendors, employees or other people.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device details, request and security logs, and the essential session information needed to keep you signed in.

Please upload only the records your review needs. We do not ask for payment card details, passwords for other services, or unrelated sensitive records such as health or identity documents.

2. How we use information

We use information to sign you in, provide the features you use, process and display your records, produce the findings and exports you ask for, support you, protect the service, investigate incidents, administer our agreements and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use your accounting records to train AI models.

3. Connected accounting systems

If you connect an accounting system such as QuickBooks Online, Cerdify reads your company information, chart of accounts, transactions and standard reports (such as the trial balance and profit and loss) for the periods you choose. We use this data only to provide your review in Cerdify.

If a reviewer in your organization approves a proposed correcting journal entry, Cerdify posts that entry to your accounting system and reads it back to confirm it was recorded as approved. Cerdify never posts without that approval.

We store the access credentials your accounting system issues to Cerdify in encrypted form. We never see your accounting-system password. You can disconnect at any time in Cerdify's settings; we then delete the stored credentials and revoke them with the provider. Data already imported stays in your projects until you delete it.

We do not share data from your accounting system with other customers or with third parties, other than the service providers listed below that run Cerdify for us.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your records while your account is active and for as long as our agreement with you requires. When you delete a project, it moves to a recycle bin for 30 days, after which it is permanently deleted from the live service. You can also delete a project permanently straight away.

Encrypted backups are kept separately so that we can recover from failures. During the beta, backups are kept for up to five years, so a deleted project can remain in a backup after it has been removed from the live service. Backups are not used for any other purpose. If a backup is ever restored, we reapply your deletions. Some records of who reviewed or approved what are kept with the history of a review, because they are part of its audit trail.

7. Your choices and requests

You can export your data and delete your projects in the app. To ask for access, correction, deletion or closing your account, write to support@cerdify.com. We will confirm your identity and authority before acting, and respond within the time the law requires. If your information is in records that one of our customers uploaded, please contact that customer first; we will help them respond.

8. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, encrypted storage of accounting-system credentials, access limited to each customer's own projects, and monitoring of the service. No online service can promise perfect security, and Cerdify does not currently hold a security certification.

9. Who the service is for

Cerdify is for businesses and their authorized adult users, starting in the United States. It is not directed to children.

10. Changes

We will post any change here with a new effective date, and tell account holders about material changes before they take effect.
